# NeuroPath — Product Spec

## Users
High school students in Indonesia who are confused about their future careers and educational paths after graduation, plus school counselors (guru BK) supporting them.

## Product Purpose
To conduct real-time AI-driven interviews that analyze student interests and aptitudes, generating a personalized, actionable roadmap (Action Plan) for their future — positioned as a digital career counseling (Bimbingan Konseling / BK) solution in the education ecosystem.

## Core Capabilities
- AI voice/text career interview with cognitive profiling (6 dimensions + archetype)
- Personalized career roadmap with granular milestones
- Counseling journal (CRUD) for tracking student guidance notes
- Unified search across journal, roadmap, and live job listings
- AI resume/portfolio, cover letter, and learning resource generation
- Blockchain credential (NFT) as proof of completion

## Brand Personality
Clean, editorial, minimalist, aesthetic. The UI should feel like a high-end editorial magazine with a strong focus on typography and whitespace (true white background, crisp high-contrast text, serif and sans-serif pairings).

## Anti-references
Dark mode, neon colors, glassmorphism, glowing orbs, cluttered dashboards, "slop" AI aesthetics (e.g., tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows everywhere).

## Design Principles
- Show, do not just tell: Use dynamic visualizers and micro-animations to make the AI feel alive.
- Action-oriented: The end result is a clear checklist, not just an abstract recommendation.
- Premium feel: High-quality typography and strategic use of color and glassmorphism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear contrast for text against dark backgrounds.
- Text-to-speech support for the AI interview.
- Easy navigation and reduced cognitive load for stressed students.
